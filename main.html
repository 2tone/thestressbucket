About the stress bucket

Stressors (Filling the Bucket)

Everyday pressures—like work issues, money worries, housing concerns, and family stress are unavoidable. They constantly pour into your bucket. If too much builds up without a release, the bucket overflows, leading to overwhelm and burnout.

Unhealthy Coping (Recycling the Pressure)

Habits like isolation, poor sleep, stress eating, or using alcohol and drugs might feel like quick relief. However, they work like a tap that simply returns the water right back into the bucket, meaning the stress never actually leaves.

Healthy Coping (Draining the Bucket)

Positive habits—like exercise, hobbies, good sleep, balanced eating, and taking prescribed medication act as a release valve at the bottom of the bucket. They safely drain the stress away, freeing up space so you can handle whatever life brings next.
